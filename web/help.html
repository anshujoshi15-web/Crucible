Crucible help

Set up your wallet, submit a Seed, and recover a pending submission.

Connect your wallet

Open Crucible in a browser with an Ethereum wallet extension. Choose Connect wallet and switch to Base Mainnet. You need EVO for the deposit and ETH on Base for gas.

Submit a Seed

Choose Drop a Seed and fill in the title, problem, and proposed mechanism. Choose Submit to Incubator. If your wallet asks for approval, wait for it to confirm and select Submit to Incubator again. Review and sign the Seed transaction in your wallet.

Recover a pending submission

Use the same browser to reopen the draft. Once the transaction confirms, choose Submit to Incubator again to verify the saved transaction hash. If prompted, paste the submission transaction hash from your wallet.

Invite a collaborator

Give the collaborator viewer access to the Site, then copy the invitation link from the Seed workspace and send it to them.
