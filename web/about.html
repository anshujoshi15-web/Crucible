About Crucible

Crucible is a private workspace for developing ideas together.

Start with a Seed

Describe a problem, how you would solve it, and the roles you need. Submitting creates an on-chain Seed ID on Base and stores the proposal in a private workspace.

Build through Branches

Invite collaborators to propose specifications, designs, code, or distribution plans. Review contributions and use weights to model how the builder pool is allocated.

From incubation to graduation

The on-chain registry gives the House separate controls to accept Seeds, reserve and approve milestones, and graduate a project.
